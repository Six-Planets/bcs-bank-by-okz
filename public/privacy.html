Privacy Policy

OZ's BCS Bank is a BCS exam-preparation app by Obide Zihad. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you use Google Sign-In, your name and profile picture provided by Google.
  • Study data: your display name, target BCS, settings, topic mastery progress, and mock-test results (scores, subjects, time taken).

Guest mode keeps all data only on your device.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, sync your progress across devices, show your personal statistics, and show the leaderboard (display name, XP and best score only). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication). The website is hosted on Vercel. Access to your records is restricted to your own account by row-level security.

Google user data

We request only the basic email, profile and openid scopes to sign you in. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in Settings, sign out at any time, or ask us to delete your account and all associated data by emailing obidezihad04@gmail.com.
